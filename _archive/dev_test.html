Casa Gonda

📍 Cap d'Artrutx, Menorca

Description

Terraced house in a private community in Cap d'Artrutx, one of the most spectacular spots on the south coast of Menorca. Designed for guests seeking privacy, comfort and tranquility by the sea.

The property offers a spacious bedroom, a full bathroom, a bright living‑dining room with direct access to the porch, a fully equipped kitchen with modern appliances, and an outdoor area with covered porch and rooftop sun terrace. Just a few minutes' walk from the Artrutx lighthouse and dreamy coves.
