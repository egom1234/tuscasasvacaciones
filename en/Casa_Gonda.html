Casa Gonda

📍 Cap d'Artrutx, Menorca

Description

Terraced house in a residential community in Cap d'Artrutx, one of the most spectacular spots in southern Menorca. Designed for families and groups looking for privacy, comfort, and peace by the sea.

The property features a spacious bedroom, a full bathroom, a bright living-dining room with direct access to the porch, a fully equipped kitchen with modern appliances, and an outdoor area with a covered porch and solarium. Just a few minutes' walk from Artrutx lighthouse and dream-like coves.
