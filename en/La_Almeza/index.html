Description

Aldea Casa Pepita is a special corner to enjoy the silence and calm of a beautiful hamlet. From the moment you step in, it awakens the sense of taste: the kitchen delivers intense flavours, and every corner surprises with careful details and labyrinthine spaces.

It has a work area overlooking the courtyard, plus a jacuzzi and sauna to unwind after a day of exploring.

Located in La Almeza (Alpuente), by the Sierra de Javalambre, perfect for walks in nature. A vehicle is recommended.

The surroundings

La Almeza belongs to the municipality of Alpuente, in the Los Serranos region of Valencia province. At over 900 metres above sea level, the landscape is defined by pine forests, holm oaks and Mediterranean scrubland. Silence, clean air and dark skies are the true luxury here.

  • Medieval castle of Alpuente (12th century)
  • Benagéber reservoir — swimming and kayaking
  • Hiking and mountain biking trails
  • Wildlife watching
  • River fishing
  • 90 minutes from Valencia city
